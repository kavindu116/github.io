ZeroHunger

Most recent

A food donation platform that moves surplus meals from donors to people who need them, with volunteers handling delivery in between. Android client, Spring Boot API, and an AI check that confirms the food actually arrived.

  • Built three separate experiences on one codebase — donor, volunteer and recipient — with role-aware routing driven by the JWT claims returned at login.
  • Wrote a Spring Boot 3 REST API secured with JWT and Spring Security, backed by PostgreSQL with Flyway handling every schema change.
  • Used Google Gemini to verify delivery proof photos, so a completed handover is confirmed by the image rather than a checkbox.
  • Android side on MVVM with Retrofit and ViewBinding, plus Lottie transitions and proper keyboard/inset handling for edge-to-edge on Android 15+.
  • Donation images stored in Firebase Storage, keeping large payloads off the API and out of the database.
